## Purpose

This file stores two different things:

1. **Observed historical baselines** from Meta Ads.
2. **Approved future KPI thresholds** once the business objective and measurement system are confirmed.

Observed baselines are not automatic targets.

## Benchmark update rule

Update this file when:
- enough historical data exists to establish a better baseline;
- unit economics change;
- conversion definition changes;
- attribution materially changes;
- the business objective changes.
