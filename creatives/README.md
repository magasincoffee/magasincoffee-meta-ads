# Creative Library

This folder stores durable creative strategy records, not necessarily the media binaries themselves.

## Folders

- `concepts/`: structured creative concepts and angles.
- `copy/`: reusable ad copy, hooks, headlines, and CTA variants.
- `asset-index.md`: index of important creative assets and their live usage.

## Rule

When a creative goes live, link the creative concept to the relevant campaign and record any known Meta ad/ad creative identifiers in the campaign record or asset index.
