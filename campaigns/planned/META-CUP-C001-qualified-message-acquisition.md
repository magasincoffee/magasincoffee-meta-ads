# META-CUP-C001 — Qualified Message Acquisition

Status: Planned / Not approved for launch
Brand: Magasin Cup
Last updated: 2026-09-28

## Business objective

Generate qualified sales opportunities for Magasin Cup.

## Proposed Meta objective

Engagement / Messaging, subject to final live setup verification.

## Hypothesis

A product-specific ad with a clear price anchor, printing benefit, and structured Messenger qualification will produce more sales-ready conversations than generic engagement campaigns.

## Qualification concept

A lead should capture:
- cup/product type;
- quantity;
- printing/logo requirement;
- delivery location;
- purchase timeframe.

Final qualification rule requires approval.

## Test design

First experiment should test one primary variable only.

Recommended order:
1. creative/offer angle;
2. then geography;
3. then product segment.

Do not test all three simultaneously.

## Audience

Initial hypothesis:
- beverage shop/business buyers;
- relevant geography;
- retargeting separated from prospecting.

Exact Meta targeting is not yet approved.

## Creative pillars

- exact product + price;
- logo printing result;
- free/custom design;
- real sample proof;
- shipping/availability.

## Budget

Not approved.

## Primary KPI

Cost per qualified lead/message.

## Supporting KPIs

- qualification rate;
- CTR;
- CPC;
- CPM.
