# Magasin Coffee — Advertising Strategy

Status: Draft — not approved for launch
Last updated: 2026-09-28

## Strategic problem

The current evidence shows awareness delivery, but not whether ads create incremental store visits, orders, qualified messages, or repeat purchases.

The next phase should connect paid media to one explicit business outcome.

## Proposed objective hierarchy

### Primary
To be approved.

Candidate outcomes:
- in-store purchase / offer redemption;
- online order;
- qualified message/order inquiry;
- measurable local demand action.

### Supporting
Awareness and engagement may support the primary outcome but should not be the final success metric by themselves.

## Proposed funnel

### Prospecting
Reach local potential customers with one clear offer or product reason to visit/order.

### Consideration
Retarget recent engagers or video viewers with proof, menu/product detail, location convenience, or a stronger offer.

### Conversion
Use the closest reliable measurable action available:
- website/order event if verified;
- qualified message if messaging is the real conversion path;
- tracked offer redemption/store code if offline purchase is primary.

## Initial audience direction

Current creative indicates a student/value segment, but that should be treated as one testable segment rather than the entire brand audience.

Potential test dimensions:
- students;
- office/workday convenience;
- nearby residents;
- existing engagers/customers.

## Creative pillars

1. Hero product + clear price.
2. Value / portion size.
3. Convenience / takeaway.
4. Product taste and visual appeal.
5. Local proof / store context.
6. Time-of-day use case.

## Measurement rule

Do not judge the next campaign only on CPM, CTR, or CPC.

The launch plan must define:
- primary business outcome;
- cost per outcome;
- minimum observation window;
- stop condition;
- scale condition.

## Budget

No budget is approved yet.

A test budget should be assigned only after the conversion path and success metric are confirmed.
