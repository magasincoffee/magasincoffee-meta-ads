# Magasin Cup — Advertising Strategy

Status: Draft — not approved for launch
Last updated: 2026-09-28

## Strategic problem

Recent ads generate clicks and message-oriented traffic, but the current purchase event/value data cannot be trusted as verified business revenue.

The strategy should optimize for qualified sales opportunities until a reliable revenue event is available.

## Proposed primary outcome

**Qualified lead / qualified Messenger conversation**

Final qualification criteria still need approval.

A useful qualified-lead definition should include enough information for sales follow-up, for example:
- product/cup type;
- quantity;
- print/logo requirement;
- delivery location;
- buying timeframe.

## Proposed funnel

### Prospecting
Show specific product/price/printing propositions to relevant business buyers.

### Qualification
Move users into Messenger or a lead flow with a structured qualification script.

### Sales follow-up
Sales handles quote, artwork, quantity, shipping, and close.

### Retargeting
Retarget recent engagers/messages who did not convert, using proof, samples, pricing tiers, or production/shipping confidence.

## Campaign structure principle

Do not mix these questions in one test:
- geography;
- product size;
- offer/price;
- creative hook.

Choose one main variable per experiment.

## Initial segmentation directions

Potential segments:
- bubble tea / coffee shops;
- takeaway beverage businesses;
- new store openings;
- existing businesses refreshing packaging;
- buyers by geographic region.

These are hypotheses, not yet approved Meta targeting definitions.

## Creative pillars

1. Product + exact price anchor.
2. Logo-printing result.
3. Free/custom design support.
4. Real printed samples.
5. MOQ / volume economics.
6. Nationwide shipping / geographic availability.
7. Speed and production reliability.

## Measurement

Primary future metric:
- cost per qualified lead.

Supporting metrics:
- message start / lead volume;
- qualification rate;
- quote rate;
- close rate;
- revenue per qualified lead;
- CTR/CPC/CPM as diagnostics only.

## Budget

No budget is approved yet.

The first controlled test should reserve enough spend to compare one meaningful variable without fragmenting into too many ad sets.
