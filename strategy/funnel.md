# Funnel Strategy

Status: Draft

## Purpose

Define how paid Meta traffic moves from awareness to a measurable business outcome.

## Funnel stages

### 1. Prospecting
Audience: people who have not recently engaged or converted.

Goal:
- introduce Magasin Coffee;
- create demand;
- drive qualified traffic or conversion signals.

Required:
- clear offer or reason to care;
- strong creative differentiation;
- exclusions for recent converters when appropriate.

### 2. Consideration / Retargeting
Audience: recent visitors, engagers, video viewers, or other warm users.

Goal:
- reduce uncertainty;
- reinforce product, offer, location, trust, or social proof;
- move users toward conversion.

### 3. Conversion
Audience: high-intent users where sufficient signal exists.

Goal:
- complete the defined conversion action.

### 4. Retention
Audience: existing customers when a measurable first-party or platform signal is available.

Goal:
- repeat purchase;
- cross-sell;
- seasonal reactivation.

## Funnel rules

- Do not create funnel stages merely for organizational neatness.
- Every stage must have a distinct user state, message, and measurement purpose.
- Avoid excessive fragmentation when budget or signal volume is low.
- Retargeting windows must be documented in the relevant campaign file.
- Exclusions should prevent obvious audience overlap when overlap would distort testing.

## Measurement

Each stage must specify:
- primary KPI;
- supporting KPI;
- conversion event;
- expected learning period;
- decision rule.
