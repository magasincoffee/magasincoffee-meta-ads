# Budget Strategy

Status: Draft

## Budget envelope

Not yet approved.

## Principles

- Budget should be allocated to answer business questions, not simply divided evenly.
- A test must receive enough spend to generate a meaningful signal.
- Do not scale solely because CTR or CPC looks attractive.
- Do not cut solely because of short-term volatility without checking spend, conversion lag, attribution, and sample size.
- Avoid frequent budget changes that reset interpretation of a test.

## Budget layers

### Baseline
Minimum spend needed to keep approved evergreen campaigns active.

### Testing
Reserved spend for creative, audience, offer, or funnel experiments.

### Scaling
Incremental spend allocated only after a campaign or ad set meets predefined scale criteria.

## Guardrails

To be approved:
- daily account spend ceiling;
- campaign spend ceiling;
- test spend ceiling;
- maximum single-step budget increase;
- minimum observation window;
- stop-loss threshold.

## Scaling protocol

Before scaling:
1. confirm live performance;
2. confirm attribution setting;
3. confirm no tracking issue;
4. confirm the campaign still matches the documented strategy;
5. check whether performance is concentrated in one creative/audience;
6. record the reason for scaling.

## Reduction protocol

Before reducing or pausing:
1. identify the KPI breach;
2. check sample size and spend;
3. check conversion delay;
4. check delivery issues;
5. document the decision.
