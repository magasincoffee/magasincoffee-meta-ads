# Creative Strategy

Status: Draft

## Purpose

Creative testing should systematically discover which message, format, proof, and offer drive business outcomes.

## Creative variables

Possible variables:
- hook;
- customer problem;
- product benefit;
- offer;
- proof;
- format;
- visual style;
- creator/person;
- call to action;
- landing destination.

Test one major variable at a time when practical.

## Creative taxonomy

Each concept should be tagged with:
- concept ID;
- funnel stage;
- audience insight;
- hook;
- message angle;
- format;
- offer;
- CTA;
- production status;
- live ad IDs when available.

## Concept categories

Examples:
- product-led;
- problem/solution;
- offer-led;
- social proof;
- founder/story;
- location/convenience;
- ritual/lifestyle;
- comparison;
- educational;
- seasonal.

## Creative review

Evaluate creative with both:
- platform diagnostics: thumb-stop, CTR, CPC, frequency, engagement;
- business diagnostics: conversion rate, CPA, revenue/ROAS where available.

A strong platform metric without business impact is not sufficient evidence of a winning concept.

## Fatigue

Creative fatigue should be assessed using:
- rising frequency;
- declining response rate;
- worsening CPA;
- delivery concentration;
- time in market.

Do not declare fatigue from frequency alone.
